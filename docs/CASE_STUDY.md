# ATS Company Website

**Company website · Doddie Prawarjito**

## Problem

A public catalogue for a company whose products and services benefit from clear navigation and visual explanation.

## Contribution

The author reports implementing the complete project. Third-party libraries, UI foundations, and historical academic credits retain their attribution. This case study makes no claim about business impact without measured evidence.

## Design and engineering

Reusable cards, carousels, product/service constants, hash routes, and lazy-loaded pages organize the catalogue. The interface retains its licensed Now UI Kit foundation.

## Current limits

The contact backend is external to this repository. Form configuration and server-side CAPTCHA validation must be completed together. No traffic or lead-generation outcomes are claimed.

## Demonstration

Browse home, products, services, projects, and contact on desktop and mobile widths.

The README describes setup. Validation results from this preparation are recorded in the local portfolio preparation report; feature descriptions alone are not test evidence.
